# Limitations

- Sentinel-1 SAR interpretation is sensitive to acquisition geometry, vegetation, urban radar effects, noise, and thresholds.
- Results depend on the selected BEFORE and AFTER scenes.
- Dynamic World is an observational cross-check with its own temporal and classification uncertainty.
- External vector services may be incomplete, rate-limited, or temporarily unavailable.
- OSM-derived exposure indicates spatial intersection or proximity, not verified damage.
- The application does not run a hydraulic model and does not predict precipitation.
- Browser verification is required for interactive controls, map tiles, and generated PDF presentation.
